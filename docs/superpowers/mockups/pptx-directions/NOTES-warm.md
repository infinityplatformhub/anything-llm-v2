# NOTES — Warm report

Palette: paper `#E9E4DB` (warm grey-beige) / lifted paper `#F2EEE7` · ink `#241F1C` · muted `#8A8076` ·
block accent deep plum `#4B2E3D` · ochre `#9A6A12`. KPI tiles are a tonal plum→ochre ladder
`#4B2E3D #6E4550 #7A5A3E #8E6B3A`; doughnut `#4B2E3D #7A4A50 #A0714B #C09A5E #D8C2A0`.
Semantics stay off the accent: good `#3F6042`, warn `#9A6A12`, bad `#A8392C`.

Fonts: Leelawadee UI everywhere. Titles 28pt bold (cover/closing 32), KPI numbers 44pt, body 16pt,
kicker/pill labels 14pt, chart labels 11-13pt, footer 10pt. All left-aligned to a 0.7in margin.

Aesthetic risk: no white anywhere. The lightest value in the deck is the paper itself, so the plum
blocks on the cover and closing carry the whole contrast budget. If a projector crushes the low end,
the deck reads as brown-on-brown rather than warm.
